WHERE DATA MEETS INNOVATION

DEXA – Data Exploration & Analytics Club is a student-driven community focused on Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Analytics, Research, and technological innovation.

The club exists to give students room to work on things that do not fit inside a syllabus — to test an idea, break it, and try again with people who care about the same questions.

Learn emerging technologies

Sessions that keep pace with the tools and methods shaping data work right now.

Build real-world projects

Take an idea from a rough notebook to something people can actually use.

Explore AI and machine learning

Study how intelligent systems are designed, trained and evaluated.

Work with real datasets

Handle messy, imperfect data — the kind that never arrives clean.

Conduct research

Frame a question, run the experiment, and write up what you found.

Participate in hackathons

Build under pressure, in a team, against a deadline that does not move.

Attend technical workshops

Hands-on walkthroughs where you leave with working code.

Collaborate with peers

Work alongside students who are curious about the same problems.

Develop professional skills

Communicate results, defend decisions, and work the way teams do.

WHERE DATA MEETS INNOVATION

DEXA – Data Exploration & Analytics Club is a student-driven community focused on Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Analytics, Research, and technological innovation.

The club exists to give students room to work on things that do not fit inside a syllabus — to test an idea, break it, and try again with people who care about the same questions.

Learn emerging technologies

Sessions that keep pace with the tools and methods shaping data work right now.

Build real-world projects

Take an idea from a rough notebook to something people can actually use.

Explore AI and machine learning

Study how intelligent systems are designed, trained and evaluated.

Work with real datasets

Handle messy, imperfect data — the kind that never arrives clean.

Conduct research

Frame a question, run the experiment, and write up what you found.

Participate in hackathons

Build under pressure, in a team, against a deadline that does not move.

Attend technical workshops

Hands-on walkthroughs where you leave with working code.

Collaborate with peers

Work alongside students who are curious about the same problems.

Develop professional skills

Communicate results, defend decisions, and work the way teams do.
